# Buyer panel: Moro Coffee House, a specialty coffee shop with 70 seats in an office-and-shops complex at Prince Talal bin Mohammad Square (7th Circle), Amman, Jordan

**16 buy · 4 pass** (80% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 7, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- A staff discount for people who work in the complex, like a Turkish coffee or small Americano for under 1.5 JOD.
- A student or group deal for Thursday nights, like a coffee for around 1.50 JOD, or free delivery to the complex offices.
- A cheap office deal, like a Turkish coffee or small Americano under 1.50 or a loyalty card with a free cup, so I could treat myself once or twice a week without feeling it.
- A student discount or a cheap first-cup offer, like a latte for under 2 JOD, so I could try it and maybe sit there with the Wi-Fi to study.

Buyers say they would buy **2.9 times** in the first month on average.
